How it works

The right model for every message.

Routing takes milliseconds and happens entirely on device.

  1. 1

    Clear-cut cases first.

    Images, code, math, Think mode, and conversations too long for Apple's 8K window go straight to Qwen. Greetings go straight to Apple.

  2. 2

    On-device triage.

    Apple's model labels the request, such as rewriting, summarizing or factual question, using guided generation, so the label is always a valid category.

  3. 3

    Follow-ups stay put.

    Short replies like “shorter” or “why?” go to the model that wrote the previous answer.

  4. 4

    Seamless fallback.

    If the on-device model hits its context limit or declines, Qwen picks up automatically.

  5. 5

    PDFs of any length.

    Text is extracted on device, with OCR for scans. Short PDFs are read in full. For long ones, Apple's model summarizes section by section, or the most relevant pages are found for your question.

PrettyAFM runs entirely on your Mac using Apple's Foundation Models framework and Ollama. Not affiliated with Apple Inc.
